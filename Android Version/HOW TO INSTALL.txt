TEST SUBJECT SOCIAL — ANDROID
=============================

This folder contains everything you need:

  Test Subject Social.apk   the game (about 11 MB)
  HOW TO INSTALL.txt        this guide

Requirements: an Android phone or tablet with Android 7.0 or newer
(64-bit, which almost every phone from the last years is).
No account or Play Store needed; the internet is only used for online play.


1. GET THE APK ONTO THE PHONE
-----------------------------
Use whichever is easiest:

  - Send it to yourself (Google Drive, email, WhatsApp, Signal, Discord, ...)
    and open it on the phone.
  - Or connect the phone to a PC with a USB cable, choose "File transfer" on
    the phone, and copy "Test Subject Social.apk" into the phone's Download
    folder.


2. INSTALL IT
-------------
  1. On the phone, open the APK (tap it in your messages/email, or open the
     "Files" app -> Downloads -> "Test Subject Social.apk").
  2. Android will say that, for security, this app source can't install
     apps. Tap "Settings", switch on "Allow from this source", then go back.
     (This appears because the game is not from the Play Store. You only
     need to allow it once for that app.)
  3. Tap "Install".
  4. If Google Play Protect asks to scan the app, you can let it scan or
     tap "Install anyway".
  5. Tap "Open" — or find "Test Subject Social" in your app drawer.

Updating later: install the new APK the same way. Your settings and
commendations are kept.


3. PLAYING
----------
  - The game runs in landscape. Menus work by tapping the buttons.
  - In SUBJECT INTAKE (the lobby): tap an empty seat to join, tap +BOT to
    fill the other seats, then tap READY.
  - During a round: drag anywhere on the left half of the screen to move
    (a joystick appears under your thumb), tap (A) for the action and
    (B) to shove. The II button at the top pauses.
  - The phone's Back button pauses the game.
  - Bluetooth gamepads also work. Up to four players can share one device
    (touch + gamepads); empty seats are filled with bots.
  - Online with friends (PC or phone, anywhere): one player taps HOST ONLINE
    and reads out the 6-letter room code; the others tap JOIN ONLINE, type
    it, and tap a seat / press (A) in Subject Intake. Press Back twice to
    leave a friend's room.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
  - "App not installed": uninstall the old version first, then install again.
  - No "Install" button / blocked: make sure "Allow from this source" is on
    for the app you used to open the APK (Settings -> Apps -> Special app
    access -> Install unknown apps).
