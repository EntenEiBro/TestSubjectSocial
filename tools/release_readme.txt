TEST SUBJECT SOCIAL 2.0.0
=========================

Double-click "Test Subject Social.exe" to play. No installation needed.
Keep the "eos" folder next to the exe: it is needed for online play.

  F11      toggle fullscreen
  Esc      pause during a round / back in menus
  Alt+F4   quit

Player 1: W A S D to move, F = action, G = shove
Player 2: arrow keys to move, Enter = action, Right Shift = shove
Gamepads: up to 4 (left stick / A / B)
In the lobby press T to add bots to empty seats (Y removes one).

Online play: HOST ONLINE shows a 6-letter room code. Friends on a PC or an
Android phone choose JOIN ONLINE and type it. Everyone needs version 2.0.
